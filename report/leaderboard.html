How the score is built

Each account's closed trades are boiled down to a handful of interpretable metrics, each ranked against the whole population, then combined into a single 0–100 score. There is no black box — every point is traceable to real trading behaviour. Weights:

25% net profit 20% profit factor 20% consistency (return / volatility) 15% win rate 10% stop-loss discipline 10% profit per trade

Profit factor is money won ÷ money lost — above 1.0 means winners outweigh losers. Consistency rewards steady gains over one lucky home-run. Stop-loss discipline credits accounts that actually cap their downside. Only accounts with at least 50 closed trades are scored, so the numbers aren't noise.
